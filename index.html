    Make a hotspot named Coffeebreak

    iPhone

    1. Open Settings, then General, then About, then Name. Change the name to Coffeebreak.
    2. Go back to Settings and open Personal Hotspot.
    3. Turn on Allow Others to Join. Choose a password you will remember.

    Android

    1. Open Settings, then Network, then Hotspot and tethering.
    2. Open WiFi hotspot. Set the network name to Coffeebreak and choose a password.
    3. Turn the hotspot on.

    On the other devices

    1. Open the WiFi list and choose Coffeebreak. Enter the password.
    2. Open this app on each device and give each a different SIM name.
    3. Type the other SIM name and tap Call.

    Your phone with the hotspot needs a mobile data signal. Hotspot use counts against your data plan.

    About

    Coffeebreak SIM 3 is app-to-app calling. It is not a carrier SIM. It has no phone number from a mobile company and it cannot call normal phone numbers. Both people open this app and call each other by SIM name. Calls are voice only and need the internet, by WiFi or mobile data.

    To call a friend, share this link with their name filled in: add ?me=theirname to the address. To dial straight away, add ?call=name.

    Name this SIM
